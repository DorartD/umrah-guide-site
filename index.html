Umrah Guide Islam: Support

Umrah Guide Islam is an iPhone app that walks you through Umrah one step at a time, following the Prophet's ﷺ practice, with the source for every step. It is available in English and Albanian.

Contact

For questions, problems or corrections, write to dorart.disha2@gmail.com. If you are reporting a mistake in the content, please tell me which screen and which step it is on, and which hadith or source it cites. That makes it much faster to check.

Common questions

How do I change the language?

Open Settings (the gear icon at the top right of the home screen) and choose English or Shqip.

How do I show only the steps for men or for women?

Use the Men, Women and Both switch at the top of the home screen. Both is the default and hides nothing. Parts that apply to one group are marked "For men" or "For women".

How do I make the text bigger?

Settings has three text sizes. The app also follows the text size you set on your iPhone under Settings, Display and Brightness, Text Size.

How do I start again?

In Settings, use the option to clear your progress. It removes your ticks, counters and saved steps. Your language, text size and Men, Women or Both choice stay.

Where do the rulings come from?

Every ruling lists its hadith or Quran verse. Hadith show the grade that sunnah.com shows, and the app does not grade hadith itself. Where scholars differ, a short note says so. The Sources screen lists them all.

Is this a fatwa?

No. The app follows the Prophet's ﷺ practice as reported in the hadith and says where scholars differ. For your own situation, ask a scholar.

Does the app need internet?

No. All the content is in the app. You only need a connection to open a link to sunnah.com or YouTube.
