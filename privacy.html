Privacy Policy

Umrah Guide Islam, an iPhone app by Dorart Disha
Last updated: 2 November 2026

Umrah Guide Islam does not collect, store or share any personal information about you. This page explains what that means in practice.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not contain any third-party code that collects data. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

What stays on your phone

To remember your place, the app saves a few things on your iPhone only:

  • your language (English or Shqip) and text size
  • your Men, Women or Both choice
  • the items you ticked and the du'as you marked as learned
  • your tawaf and sa'i counts
  • your position in the step-by-step screens

This information never leaves your phone and I cannot see it. You can clear the ticks, counters and saved steps in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything. If you back up your iPhone, iOS may include this app data in that backup. The backup is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy, not by me.

Links to other sites

Some screens link to sunnah.com, and one links to a YouTube video. These open in Safari or the YouTube app only when you tap them. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and the app does not send them anything about you.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
